How it works

No swiping. No likes. A real plan, with a real window.

1Host a spot

Pick a public place, write the idea, and post it. A spot stays up for 72 hours and takes up to 7 requests.

2Request to join

See the plan and a few basics. Photos stay hidden until you use a reveal. If it sounds like your kind of day, send a request.

3The host chooses

The host picks one request. The spot closes, both profiles open up, and a chat starts with the plan already made.

Your safety

Built around a plan, not a profile.

  • Profiles start concealed. Before a reveal, people see your plan and a few basics, not your photos.
  • Dates are at public places. Every spot is a venue on the map.
  • Report or block anyone you meet on Idyll.
  • Photos and text are checked automatically. Checks can miss things, so report anything that gets through.
