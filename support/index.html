Support

We read everything.

Email support@joinidyll.com for help with your account, to report a safety concern, or to request a copy or deletion of your data. Include the email address on your account so we can verify it's you.

Common questions

How do I delete my account? In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. Your profile disappears immediately; see the Privacy Policy for what happens to your data.

How do I report someone? From their profile or from the chat, tap the options menu and choose Report. Every report is reviewed by a person.

Why don't I see any date spots? Idyll shows spots within the distance you've set, posted in the last 72 hours. Try widening your range in Settings. We're starting in New York City.

If you're in danger, contact local emergency services first.
