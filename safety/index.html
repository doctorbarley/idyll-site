Idyll is a way to meet people in person, and meeting someone new always carries some risk. This page explains what Idyll does and does not do, and what you can do to look after yourself.

If you are in immediate danger, call 911. Idyll support is not an emergency channel. Do not wait for a reply from us.

IDYLL DOES NOT CONDUCT CRIMINAL BACKGROUND CHECKS ON ITS USERS.

1. How Idyll works

Hosts post a real date at a real place. Anyone can request it. The host chooses who to meet. Idyll doesn't select, rank, or recommend people, and doesn't verify identities or run background checks.

  • Adults only. Idyll is for people 18 and older.
  • No criminal background checks. We do not look into anyone's criminal history.
  • No identity verification. A person may not be who their profile says they are. Anyone who is able to commit identity theft can also falsify a dating profile.
  • Automated moderation has limits. Idyll uses automated tools to check photos and text for content that breaks our rules. These tools make mistakes and can miss things.

You decide who you talk to and who you meet. Nothing on Idyll replaces your own judgment.

2. Before you meet

There is no substitute for acting with caution when communicating with any stranger who wants to meet you.

  • Take your time. You never owe anyone a date, a reply or an explanation.
  • Protect your personal information. Never include your last name, email address, home address, phone number, place of work or other identifying details in your profile or your first messages. Be just as careful with anything about your finances.
  • Stop talking to anyone who pressures you. If someone pushes you for personal or financial information, or tries to trick you into giving it, end the conversation and report them.
  • Never send money, gift cards or cryptocurrency. This holds whatever reason you are given. A request for money is a strong sign of a scam.
  • Trust your instincts. If something feels off, it is fine to stop replying, withdraw a request or cancel.

3. Meeting in person

  • Go to the posted public spot. Meet at the public place named in the date spot, with other people around.
  • Stay in public on a first meeting. Do not move to a home or any other private place.
  • Share the plan with a friend. Tell a friend or family member where you are going, who you are meeting and when you expect to be back.
  • Arrange your own transportation. Get yourself there and back. Do not agree to be picked up at your home.
  • Watch your drink. Keep it in sight, and do not accept one you did not see poured.
  • Leave if you are uncomfortable. You can leave at any time, for any reason, without explaining.

4. Reporting and blocking

In the app. On a person's profile or in a chat, open the options menu and choose Report or Block.

By email. Write to support@joinidyll.com. Include the email address on your account and what happened.

  • A report may lead to an account being suspended or removed. We cannot promise a particular outcome.
  • We do not tell the person you reported who reported them.
  • If we ban an account for fraud, we notify the people who exchanged messages with that account.
  • A report to Idyll is not a report to the police. If you think a crime has been committed, contact law enforcement as well.

5. Intimate images shared without consent

If an intimate image of you has been shared on Idyll without your consent, you can ask us to remove it. You do not need an Idyll account to make this request.

Email support@joinidyll.com with the subject line Intimate Image Removal Request. Please include:

  • Your name and how we can reach you.
  • Where the image appears on Idyll, in as much detail as you can give: the profile, date spot or chat, and the date and time you saw it.
  • A statement that you are the person in the image, or are authorized to act for that person, and that it was shared without consent.
  • Your typed full name as a signature.

Do not send the image itself in your first email. If we need more to find it, we will tell you how to send it.

These services can also help you get intimate images taken down across other platforms:

  • StopNCII.org, for adults.
  • Take It Down, from the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children, for images taken when you were under 18.

6. Crisis resources

  • Emergency: call 911.
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988, or visit 988lifeline.org.
  • Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741, or visit crisistextline.org.

7. Community expectations

  • Be 18 or older.
  • Be honest. Use your own photos and tell the truth about yourself.
  • Be respectful. No harassment, threats or hate.
  • Respect consent. A no, a pause or a change of mind is final, online and in person.
